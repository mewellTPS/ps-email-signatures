What ships today. The navy wordmark and purple endorsement sit on transparency, so on a dark background they are about 1.2:1 contrast and disappear. The navy dividers vanish too.

The wordmark changes from navy to #3A80C0, which holds about 4:1 contrast on both white and dark. No box at all. Endorsement uses the PS white pill. Changes the brand color of the wordmark.

All options also change the two divider lines from navy to #3A80C0: Outlook recolors text in dark mode but leaves border colors alone, so navy dividers disappear.
