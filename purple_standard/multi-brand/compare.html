Purple Standard signature v2 — brand-strip icon treatments

Same signature four ways. C is the selected treatment; the others are kept as demo material. The only thing changing between tabs is how the brand icons are normalized. Everything else is identical to the live v1 signature. Icons are shown at real size (40 px) in the signature, then enlarged below for inspection.

D · Hybrid. (alternative, kept for future demos.) Marks that are already circles (ACE, Purple Current, Boyette) run full-bleed and bare, so they use the whole 40 px. The marks with no field of their own get a white field and a thin brand ring: WTR DR's drop (cropped off the wordmark, which is illegible this small), Tyrian's shield and Brisa's bear. MOR and ENG sit light so they read as pending rather than as content.

Icons enlarged 2×

A · Plain discs. No rings anywhere. Cleanest in isolation, but on a white email background WTR DR and Tyrian have no visible edge — they read as loose glyphs floating beside solid discs, and the row loses its rhythm.

Icons enlarged 2×

B · Ring on all. Every icon gets a white field and a brand ring — maximum consistency, and it echoes the tps-badge's own ring. The cost is real: the four naturally-round marks become circles inside circles, which shrinks each mark by about a third and makes ACE and Boyette muddy at 40 px.

Icons enlarged 2×

C · Native shapes — selected. No shape forcing — each asset is only trimmed and scaled to fit. Honest to the source files, but it exposes why they need editing: WTR DR's "WTR DR" wordmark is unreadable mush at 40 px, and Tyrian's shield and Brisa's bear are taller than the discs, so the row's baseline wobbles.

Strip spacing — which right edge do you want?

The strip is the only block in the signature that doesn't span the full 500 px, so its right edge doesn't line up with the endorsement bar underneath it. Tight keeps the icons reading as one group (closest to your mockup). Justified spreads them so the strip and the endorsement bar share the same right edge.
